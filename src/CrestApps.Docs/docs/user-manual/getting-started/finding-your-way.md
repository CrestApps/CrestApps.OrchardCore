Everything you do in the application happens in the browser, on the **admin** pages of your company's site. This page shows you how to sign in, what each top-level menu holds, and where to change your own details. Read it first if you are new.

## Sign in

1. Open the address your administrator gave you, for example `https://crm.example.com/Admin`.
2. Enter your user name or email and your password, and sign in.
3. You land on the admin **dashboard**. The menu is on the left.

Your administrator creates your user and gives you your roles. If you cannot sign in, or you forgot your password, ask your administrator.

:::tip[Agents: sign in to the phone too]
The soft phone in the [browser extension or Windows app](../phone-apps.md) uses the same sign-in as the site. Sign in to the site in the same browser, or inside the Windows app, and the phone is ready.
:::

## The admin menu

The menu shows only what your roles allow and what your site has turned on. Two people at the same company can see very different menus. See [Roles and permissions](roles-and-permissions.md) for why.

These are the top-level items you are most likely to see:

| Menu | What lives there | Mostly used by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Interaction Center** | The CRM and the contact center: your work, contacts, the live dashboard, voicemail, and the **Management** screens where the contact center is built. | Agents, supervisors, managers |
| **Messaging** | The shared text-message **Inbox**, **Broadcasts** and message **Templates**. | Agents, supervisors |
| **Reports** | Every report, grouped by category. | Supervisors, managers |
| **Artificial Intelligence** | AI profiles, templates, the AI connections and deployments, knowledge sources, tools, and AI reports. AI profiles your administrator chose to show also appear here as chat pages. | AI content managers, administrators |
| **Content** | Content items such as pages, and the **Import** and **Export** screens for contacts and other records. Administrators also define content types here. | Managers, administrators |
| **Design** | The site's look, its widgets, and **Workflows** that automate what happens on events. | Administrators |
| **Search** | Search indexes, including the indexes the AI uses for knowledge. | Administrators |
| **Access Control** | **Users** and **Roles**. | Administrators |
| **Settings** | Site-wide settings, such as **Contact Center**, **Communication** (the phone and SMS providers) and **Artificial Intelligence**. | Administrators |
| **Tools** | **Features** (turn parts of the product on and off), **Recipes**, **Time Zones** and other utilities. | Administrators |

Your site may show other items too, depending on what it uses.

## The soft phone and the agent bar

Agents make and take calls in the **soft phone**, which runs in the [browser extension or the Windows app](../phone-apps.md). While you are signed in to queues, the **docked agent bar** sits at the bottom of every admin page and pops up when work is offered to you, so you can stay on any page. See [Agent workspace](../agent-workspace.md).

## Your own profile

The user menu at the top right of the admin pages has your own options:

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Profile** | Your own details. When your site uses them, this includes your first and last name (which colleagues see instead of your user name) and your time zone. |
| **Change Password** | Changes your password. You enter your current password first. |
| **Log off** | Signs you out of the site. |

Your **extension** number, the **queues** you may sign in to and your **roles** are set by your manager or administrator, not on your profile. You can record your own [voicemail greeting](../voicemail.md) under **Interaction Center > My voicemail greeting**.
