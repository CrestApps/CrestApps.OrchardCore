Agents open tickets for people who call, write or stop by. Customers can also open their own requests from the [Tickets Portal](./portal.md) or a [request form on a page](./web-form.md).

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Menu** | Service Desk > Tickets > New ticket |
| **Permission** | Reply to tickets on the desk |
| **Feature** | Tickets |

<AskYourAdmin />

## Steps

1. Select **New ticket** and the kind of ticket (with several ticket types, the menu lists them), or **New ticket** on an account's **Tickets** tab, which asks which kind first. The form then asks what that kind of ticket needs; to open another kind, go back and choose it.
2. Choose who it is **for**, or enter a guest's name and e-mail.
3. Enter the **Subject** and the first message, and attach files if you need to: drag them onto the form, paste them, or select **browse**. Files you add later join the ones already there, and the × next to a file removes it.
4. Choose a **Category** when the ticket type has categories, one level at a time. The category can send the ticket to its desk and team with its priority and service level. Questions that depend on the category or another answer show as you choose.
5. Choose the **Desk** and, if you want, a **Priority**.
6. If the requester gave you a login or another secret, add it under **Protected information** rather than in the message. See [Protected information](./protected-information.md).
7. Select **Publish**.

What a ticket starts with comes first from what you chose on the form, then from its category, then from the ticket type. When the desk assigns new tickets, the ticket goes to a member of its team. See [Desks](./desks.md).

## From a text conversation

With the messaging workspace on, each conversation row has **Open a ticket**. It opens **New ticket** with the customer as requester, a subject and the latest messages of the conversation as the first message. The new ticket links back to the conversation.
