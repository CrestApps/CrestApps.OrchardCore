Time you spend on tickets is part of your day in **My time**, with no second timesheet to fill.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Menu** | Service Desk > Tickets, then a ticket's **Time** panel |
| **Permission** | **Reply to tickets** on the ticket's desk |
| **Feature** | Tickets and Time Tracker |

<AskYourAdmin />

The **Time** panel shows when your administrator has chosen a **Time on tickets** stage for the desk. See [Desks](./desks.md#time-on-tickets).

## Start and stop a timer

Select **Start working on this ticket** when you pick it up and **Stop working on this ticket** when you're done; your time goes back to what you were doing before. Starting another ticket ends the first one's entry. The panel shows how long each person worked on the ticket.

## Log time after the fact

Forgot the timer? Select **Log time**, then:

1. Enter the **Time spent** (`30`, `45m`, `1h 30m` or `1:30`).
2. Enter when you **Started** (leave it empty if you just finished).
3. Enter a **Note** on what you did.
4. When the desk also has a stage for **Time not billed to the customer**, clear **Billable** for time the customer isn't charged for.
5. Select **Log time**.

If you were clocked in, the time moves from what you were doing to the ticket, so your day still adds up.

## Correct an entry

**Entries** lists every entry on the ticket with its note. To correct or remove an entry, ask someone who may edit everyone's time; they use **Time entries** in Time Tracker.

With [agreements](../administration/agreements.md) on, the billable time of a ticket counts against the hours of the agreement it works under.
