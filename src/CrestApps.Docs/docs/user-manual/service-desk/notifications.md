What happens on your tickets reaches you in the notification center (the bell at the top of the admin) and by e-mail, or the other ways you chose. Each notice names the ticket, with its desk, and opens it. You aren't told about your own changes, and a notice about a ticket you may no longer see disappears from your list.

| Notification | When you get it |
| --- | --- |
| **Tickets assigned to you** | Someone else, or the desk, gives you a ticket |
| **Tickets given to your team** | A ticket goes to a team you are on (the agent who gets it hears of it as theirs instead) |
| **Replies from requesters** | The requester replies on your ticket, or on your team's ticket that nobody has yet |
| **Internal notes on your tickets** | Someone else adds a note to your ticket (a note that mentions you comes as a mention instead) |
| **Mentions** | Someone writes @ and your user name in a note or reply, on a ticket or any other record you may read |
| **Status changes on your tickets** | Someone else, or a workflow, moves your ticket to another status (not when the requester's reply reopens it: that comes as the reply) |
| **Ticket approvals** | A request waits for your approval |
| **Service-level targets** | A target of your ticket, or of your team's ticket that nobody has yet, is due soon or missed; once for each, until you read it |
| **Tickets merged into yours** | Another ticket is merged into your ticket |
| **Ticket workflow notices** | A workflow your administrator set up tells you about a ticket |
| **Your requests received** | For requesters who sign in to the site: a request you sent is received, with its number |
| **Replies to your requests** | For requesters who sign in to the site: an agent replies on your request |
| **Your requests solved** | For requesters who sign in to the site: a request you sent is solved |
| **Rating your solved requests** | For requesters who sign in to the site: you are asked how a solved request was handled |
| **Agreement hours**, **Agreements ending** | For an account's manager: an agreement used 80% or all of its hours, or is about to end |

## Choose how you get each one

1. Open **Profile** from the user menu at the top right. If you may not edit your own profile, ask your administrator.
2. Use **Notification Types**: clear a type's box under a method, such as **Email Notifications**, to stop getting it that way.
3. Save.

The notification center still lists every notice, so nothing is lost. A desk can stop assignment and team notices for all its tickets with **Tell the assignee** in its settings. See [Desks](./desks.md).

What requesters get, and the words of those messages, are set under **Settings > Service Desk**. See [Notification templates](./notification-templates.md).
