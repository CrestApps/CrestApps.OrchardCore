## Merge a duplicate

**Merge** moves a duplicate into another ticket by its number. The duplicate becomes read only and points to the ticket it was merged into. Its files, and its protected information, move to the ticket it's merged into, and the assignee of that ticket is told.

To merge several at once, select them in the ticket list and choose **Merge**: they go into the oldest one.

## Link tickets and records

**Links** relate a ticket to another ticket or to any record, such as an order or a work order, with a relation such as **Related** or **Duplicate**. To link two tickets you must be able to see both.

## Problems and incidents

When many people report the same outage, open one ticket for the cause and link each report to it with **Its problem** under **Links**. The cause's page lists its incidents (the ones you may see by name, the others counted), and solving it solves every open incident, telling each requester. An incident already solved, merged or only related stays as it is.

## Follow-ups

A closed ticket is read only. When the requester replies after it closed, their reply opens a new ticket that follows up the closed one, so the conversation goes on without reopening finished work.
