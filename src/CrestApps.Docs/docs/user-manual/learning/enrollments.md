An enrollment is one person taking one course. It keeps their progress, why they have access, and every change made to it, with who made it and why.

| **Permission** | View enrollments; Manage enrollments to change them; View learning reports; Export learner records to export. Instructors, assistants and graders named on a course (with Manage own courses) see **Learning > Enrollments** for their own courses, and instructors and assistants can **Enroll a learner** in them. |
| **Feature** | Learning; Reports for the reports; Audit Trail to record each change |

## Enroll a learner

1. Go to **Learning > Enrollments** and select **Enroll a learner**.
2. Search for the **Learner** and choose the **Course**.
3. Under **Dates**, set when **Access ends** and when the course is **Due**, in your time zone, or leave them empty.
4. Tick **Skip prerequisites** to enroll someone who has not completed the courses this course asks for first.
5. Select **Enroll**.

Enrolling someone who is already enrolled in the course changes nothing.

## Find enrollments

The list shows each enrollment's learner, course, status and progress. Use the search box and the course and status filters above the list.

## Follow a learner

Open an enrollment to see:

- **Progress**: each lesson by section, its state, the time spent, the score, and how it was completed. A lesson the browser reported is marked so.
- **Access grants**: why the person has access, such as enrolled by hand, with a key, or by a data pipeline.
- **History**: every change of status, with when, who and why.

The person's enrollments are also on their user page and, for staff, on the **Learning** section of their staff profile.

## Change an enrollment

At the bottom of an enrollment:

- **Approve** or **Reject** a request to join a course that needs approval. Rejecting needs a reason. The course's instructors and assistants get a notification when someone asks to join; for a course without them, the people who may manage every enrollment, and the administrators, get it.
- **Mark complete** on a lesson completes it for the learner, with a reason, such as training done on paper.
- **Approve the completion** of a course that needs a staff member to approve it.
- **Change the status, with a reason**: suspend, make active again, mark completed or withdraw.
- **Change the dates, with a reason**: extend access or move the due date.

Each change is kept in the history and, with Audit Trail on, in the Audit Trail.

## Reviews after completion

If a quiz result a completed enrollment rests on is voided or lowered later, the enrollment stays completed and shows **Needs review**. Filter the list by **Needs review**, open the enrollment, decide what follows (such as booking a retake) and **Clear the review** with a note. The course's instructors get a notification when a review is needed.

Clearing the review checks the completion again: if the course awards a digital credential or education credits, or counts toward a learning path or a certification, the learner gets what the completion earns, once. Something they already got before the review is not given twice.

## Reminders

Learners get a notice when they are enrolled (also when you approve their request), when you decline their request, a few days before a course is due, and when it is overdue. Each learner can switch each notice off, or also get it by e-mail or text message, in their notification preferences: **Choose how each kind of notification reaches you** at the bottom of their **Notifications** page, without the admin.

Learners read their notices on your site, without the admin: the bell at the top of the page shows the unread ones, and **Notifications** in the menu under their name lists them all. Selecting a notice opens what it is about, such as the course, and marks it read.

## Reports

Go to **Reports**:

- **Course completion**: per course, how many people enrolled in the period, how many are in progress, completed, expired or withdrawn, the completion rate and the average grade.
- **Learner transcript**: the courses completed in the period, for one learner (enter their user name) or for everyone.
- **Inactive learners**: people taking a course who have not opened it for two weeks or more.

Select **Export** for a file of what you see.
