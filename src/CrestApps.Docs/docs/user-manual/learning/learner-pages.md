Learners take courses on your site, on a phone or a desktop.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Menu** | My courses in the user menu; My learning and the other learner pages in Your account when the My Account feature is on |
| **Permission** | Access learning (every signed-in person by default) |
| **Feature** | Learner Pages; Self Enrollment for the Enroll button; My Account for the Your account area |

## Enroll in a course

Open the course page. Under its title, the top of the page shows the course's summary, its code, level, length, number of required lessons, categories and dates, its cover picture if it has one, and the one thing to do next. Depending on the course, that is:

- **Enroll**, to start at once;
- an **Enrollment key** box for the key your trainer gave you;
- **Ask to enroll**, after which a training manager approves you. Until then the page says your request waits for approval.

A course your organization enrolls you in says so and has no button. A lesson marked **Free preview** can be opened before enrolling. Once you are enrolled, the same place shows your progress and **Continue where you left off**, with the lesson you continue with; once you completed the course, it shows **You completed this course**. The description and the course outline follow. A course that is not for sale shows no price box.

## Take the course

Select **Start the course** or **Continue** on the course page, or open the course from **My learning**. The player shows the course outline beside the lesson on a desktop and under it on a phone. Above the lesson, a line says what kind of lesson it is, its section, where it is in the course (*3 of 8*) and about how long it takes. **Previous** and **Next** move through the lessons. Text between lessons shows with the lesson it belongs to, never in the outline. Select **Collapse all** above the outline to fold its sections, as in Moodle's course index; the section you are in stays open. A lesson that is not open yet says when it opens, or which lesson to finish first, with a button to go to it.

Each lesson says what completes it, under its title in the player and beside it in the course page's outline, as Moodle's completion conditions do: *To do: Mark it as done*, *To do: Watch 80% of the video*, *To do: Pass with 70%*, *To do: Confirm the statement*. It turns into *Done: ...* once you meet it, and *Not passed: ...* after a result below the pass mark. Optional lessons say **Optional**. The outline also shows each lesson's kind and about how long it takes.

- A page counts when you open it, or when you select **Mark as done**, as the course says.
- A video counts when you have watched enough of it.
- An acknowledgement counts when you tick the statement and select **Confirm**.

**Course progress** shows how far you are, as *3 of 7 complete · 43%* under the bar, and each section of the outline shows how many of its required lessons you completed. When every required lesson is done, the course is completed and the player shows **You completed this course**: the date, your grade, links to what the completion earned (a certificate or a digital credential, when your site issues them) and to **My transcript**. If the course completes while a lesson is open (for example when a video reaches its share), a message under the lesson leads to it. The course page shows the same panel, and **Finish** on the last lesson opens it again. You can still review the course, and do the lessons you had not done, such as the feedback survey at the end or an optional lesson: they show *Done* once you finish them, while the course stays completed as it was, with the same date and grade.

## Find a course

The **Course catalog**, linked from **My learning**, lists the published courses by title. Search the titles, or choose a category on the left to see its courses and those of its sub-categories. Each course shows its code, its categories, its dates and its summary; select it to open its page and enroll.

## My learning and the transcript

**My courses**, in the menu under your name at the top of every page, lists the courses you are taking. Select a course to open it where you left off, or **My courses** (or **See all**, when you take more than five) to open **My learning**.

**My learning** shows a card for each of your courses, as Moodle's course overview does: the course's picture (or a colored pattern with its initials), its category, title and code, your progress (*3 of 7 complete · 43%*), its due date and **Start**, **Continue** or **Review**. Filter the cards with **All**, **In progress**, **Not started** and **Completed** (each with its count), search your courses by title or code, and switch between **Cards** and **List** with the buttons beside the search. A course that has not started yet says when it starts; a course waiting for approval or paused says so. A course whose end date has passed says **Ended on**: it stays open, so you can still finish it. **My transcript** lists every course you completed, as it was when you completed it, with the date, the grade and the time you spent on its lessons and quizzes (*Less than a minute*, *12 min*, *1 h 05 min*).

When the **Your account** area is on (the Users **My Account** feature, which the store, tickets and other features also turn on; no store is needed), these pages open inside it, with its menu on the left: **My learning**, **My transcript**, **My required learning**, **My certifications**, **My education credits** and **My credentials**. If you are a member of a business account, **Account members** lists its members; an admin of the account also sees **Course seats**, to give the seats the account bought.
