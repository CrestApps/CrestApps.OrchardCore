Learners take courses on your site, on a phone or a desktop.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Menu** | My courses in the user menu; My learning in My account when the store is on |
| **Permission** | Access learning (every signed-in person by default) |
| **Feature** | Learner Pages; Self Enrollment for the Enroll button |

<AskYourAdmin />

## Enroll in a course

Open the course page. It shows the outline and, depending on the course:

- **Enroll**, to start at once;
- an **Enrollment key** box for the key your trainer gave you;
- **Ask to enroll**, after which a training manager approves you. Until then the page says your request waits for approval.

A course your organization enrolls you in says so and has no button. A lesson marked **Free preview** can be opened before enrolling.

## Take the course

Select **Start the course** or **Continue** on the course page, or open the course from **My learning**. The player shows the course outline beside the lesson on a desktop and above it on a phone. **Previous** and **Next** move through the lessons. A lesson that is not open yet says when it opens.

- A page counts when you open it, or when you select **Mark as done**, as the course says.
- A video counts when you have watched enough of it.
- An acknowledgement counts when you tick the statement and select **Confirm**.

**Course progress** shows how far you are. When every required lesson is done, the course is completed and the page says when you completed it. You can still review it.

## My learning and the transcript

**My courses**, in the menu under your name at the top of every page, lists the courses you are taking. Select a course to open it where you left off, or **My courses** (or **See all**, when you take more than five) to open **My learning**.

**My learning** lists the courses you are taking, with your progress and due dates. **My transcript** lists every course you completed, as it was when you completed it, with the date and grade.
