A **learning path** puts courses in order: people finish one stage before the next opens. **Required learning** asks a group of people to complete a course, a path or a certification by a due date, reminds them, and tells their manager when it is overdue.

| **Permission** | Manage required learning; Exempt people from required learning to exempt someone; See the required learning of the people who report to you for managers |
| **Feature** | Learning Paths; Required Learning; Team Learning for managers |

## Create a learning path

1. Open **Content > New > Learning Path** and give it a title.
2. Under **Course sets**, add a set for each group of courses. For each set, choose the courses (type part of a title to find one) and how many must be completed: **Every course**, **Any one course** or **At least** a number.
3. Choose how each set joins the one before it: **Then** opens it once everything before is done; **And** and **Or** keep it open alongside the set before. Click **Add a set** for more, or the bin to remove one.
4. Tick **People can start the path themselves** to show a **Start this path** button on the path's page.
5. Click **Publish**.

People on a path are enrolled in the courses of the stage that is open to them; when they complete it, the next stage's courses are added to **My courses**. To see who is on a path, open the path's actions in the content list and choose **People**; you can also start someone there by user name.

## Require learning

First define who it is for: an [audience](../administration/users#audiences) such as "everyone in the Field team hired more than 30 days ago". Then open **Learning > Required Learning** and click **Add required learning**.

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | The name people see. |
| **What is required** | A course, a learning path or a certification path, then which one. |
| **Audience** | Everyone in the audience must complete it, including people who join it later. |
| **People** | User names required besides the audience. |
| **Due** | No due date, a date, some days after it is required, or some days after the hire date. |
| **Reminders** | Days before the due date to remind the person, such as 14, 7, 1. |
| **Tell the manager** | Days after the due date to tell the person's manager. |
| **On** | When off, the rule requires nobody new. |

Click **Save and run now** to apply it at once, or **Save** to let it run within the hour. Each rule runs again once a day.

On the **People** page of a rule you see each person's state: **Not started**, **In progress**, **Overdue**, **Completed**, **Exempt** or **No longer required**. **Who it covers** lists who the rule covers today before anything changes, and **Run now** applies it.

### Exempt someone

When a person already has the learning, open the rule's **People**, choose **Exempt** in their actions, give the reason and, if you have one, attach the evidence. Only the person and people who can exempt can open the file. **Take the exemption back** requires the learning again.

## What learners see

**My required learning**, in the **Your account** menu, lists what is required of the person with its due date and state, and the learning paths they are on with the stage they are at. Reminders and overdue notices arrive as notifications.

## See your team

Managers open **Learning > Team Learning** to see the required learning of the people who report to them, with what is overdue first. It shows states and due dates only, never answers.
