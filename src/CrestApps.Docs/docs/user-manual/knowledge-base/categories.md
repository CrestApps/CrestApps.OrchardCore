Categories group articles about the same thing, such as *Accounts* or *Billing*. The help center lists the articles of each category together, and each category has its own page.

| | |
| --- | --- |

| **Feature** | Knowledge Base |

## Add or arrange categories

The categories are the terms of the **Knowledge Base Categories** taxonomy.

1. Go to **Content** → **Content Items** and open **Knowledge Base Categories**.
2. Add terms, such as *Accounts* and *Billing*. Drag a term under another one to make it a sub-category.
3. Publish the taxonomy.

Then give each article its **Category** when you write it. See [Write articles](./write-articles.md).

## How readers see them

- The help center lists each category with its most helpful articles and a link to the rest.
- The list of categories shows how many articles each holds, counting those of its sub-categories. It only counts the articles the reader may open, and leaves out a category with none.
- A category's page lists its articles and those of its sub-categories, and its search box searches within it.
- On an article's page, the article's category is picked in the list of categories.
