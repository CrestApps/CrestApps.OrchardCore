Surveys lets you build surveys, polls, forms and quizzes, share them, and read the results. Use it for customer satisfaction and Net Promoter Score surveys, employee engagement, event feedback, registrations, polls, inspections and graded quizzes.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Menu** | Surveys > Surveys |
| **Permission** | Manage own surveys, or Manage all surveys |
| **Feature** | Surveys |

<AskYourAdmin />

## What you can do

- [Create a survey](./create-a-survey.md) from a template or from a blank page.
- [Add questions](./add-questions.md) of many types: choices, text, numbers, dates, ratings, grids, rankings, file uploads and more.
- [Add logic](./logic.md): show a question only when an earlier answer calls for it, jump to a later page, or end the survey early.
- [Build a quiz](./quizzes.md) with points, a pass mark, a time limit and attempts.
- Reuse questions from [question banks](./question-banks.md).
- [Publish](./publish-and-versions.md) the survey; people who already started keep the version they started on.
- [Share it](./share-and-invite.md) with a link, a QR code, a kiosk, personal invitations or a page of your site.
- [View the results](./view-results.md), [grade answers](./grade-responses.md) and [export the responses](./export.md).
- [Translate](./translations.md) the survey, and get [AI help](./ai-help.md).

## The Surveys menu

| Item | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Surveys** | The list of surveys, with their designer, links, responses and results |
| **Question banks** | Questions you reuse in many surveys and quizzes |
| **Grading** | Answers that wait for a person to score them |

Reports are under **Reports > Surveys**. The defaults for new surveys are under **Settings > Surveys**.
