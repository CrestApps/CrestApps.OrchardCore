The invoicing and billing features bill your customers and collect what they owe. You send invoices and estimates, keep track of every balance, remind customers before and after payments fall due, charge late fees, and bill on a schedule. Customers see and pay their invoices in their account, online. You can use them on their own, without an online store, or together with the store.

## What each feature does

| Feature | What it does for your business | Page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Invoicing** | Create invoices and credit notes with line items and tax, issue them with a number, send them as PDFs, take payments, void or write them off. Estimates, deposits, payment schedules, approvals, billing expenses and time, usage, late fees, statements and recurring invoices are part of it. | [Invoicing](commerce/invoicing.md) |
| **Transactions** | See every balance a customer owes, from an invoice, an order, Pay Later or a payment plan; record payments, adjust or waive amounts, and remind customers on a schedule. | [Transactions](commerce/transactions.md) |
| **Invoices and receipts** | The receipts customers get when they pay, and the simple invoices the site makes for balances that have no Invoicing invoice. | [Invoices and receipts](commerce/invoices-and-receipts.md) |
| **Payment terms** | Keep the terms you give customers, such as *Net 30*, *End of month* or a discount for paying early, so invoices and Pay Later work out due dates the same way. | [Payment terms](commerce/payment-terms.md) |
| **Document templates and number series** | Choose how invoices and other documents look and how they are numbered. | [Document templates and number series](commerce/document-templates-and-number-series.md) |
| **Exchange rates** | Bill in other currencies and report totals in your own. | [Exchange rates](commerce/exchange-rates.md) |

| **Installment plans** | Split a large amount into payments, charged to a card or invoiced on each due date. | [Installment plans](commerce/installment-plans.md) |

## How a bill is paid

1. **You bill the customer.** An invoice you issue, a recurring invoice, a subscription's renewal or a purchase paid later with Pay Later each leave an amount owed, with a due date from the customer's payment terms.
2. **The customer is told.** The invoice is e-mailed with its PDF, and the customer sees it under **Invoices** and **Balances and invoices** in their account. Reminders go out before and after it falls due.
3. **The customer pays.** Online from their account or the link in the e-mail, or you record a payment they made another way. A late payment can have a late fee added.
4. **It's settled.** The invoice is marked paid, the customer gets a receipt, and the amount shows in your reports.

Taxes, payment providers such as Stripe and Pay Later, and refunds are shared with the store; see [Taxes](commerce/taxes.md), [Payment providers](commerce/payment-providers.md) and [Payments and refunds](commerce/payments-and-refunds.md).
