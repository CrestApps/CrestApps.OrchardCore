The words this manual uses, in plain language. Each term links to the page that explains it.

## A

- **A2A (Agent-to-Agent)**: an open standard that lets AI agents work together. Your site can call AI agents hosted elsewhere, and can offer its own AI profiles to other systems. See [Tools and agents](ai/tools-and-agents.md).
- **Account**: the company or household that contacts and opportunities belong to. See [Leads, accounts and opportunities](leads-accounts-opportunities.md).
- **Activity**: one piece of work for one contact: a call to make, a text to send, or an inbound call to log. Agents complete activities with a disposition. See [Activities](activities.md).
- **Activity load**: a batch that creates many activities at once from a filtered list of contacts. A load is **Manual** (for agents), **Automatic** (for the AI) or **Dialer** (for the outbound dialer). See [Load activities](load-inventory.md).
- **Agent**: a person who takes and places calls and answers messages. Also the name of a built-in role. See [Roles and permissions](getting-started/roles-and-permissions.md).
- **Agent bar**: see *Docked agent bar*.
- **Agent entitlement**: see *Entitlement*.
- **Agent state**: why an agent is not taking work, such as *Lunch* or *Team meeting*. Also called a reason code. Each one maps to a presence such as Break, Away or Meeting. See [Agent states](agent-states.md).
- **AI agent**: an AI profile that holds a conversation with a customer by itself, by text or on a call. See [Automated AI SMS and voice](automated-ai.md).
- **AI profile**: a configured AI assistant: its instructions, the AI model it uses, its knowledge and its tools. Chat, the website widget and AI agents all run on a profile. See [AI profiles](ai/profiles.md).
- **Automated activity**: an activity the AI works on its own, created by an automatic load or by the AI answering a number. See [Automated AI SMS and voice](automated-ai.md).

## B

- **Barge**: a supervisor joins a live call, and both the agent and the customer hear them. See [Live dashboard](live-dashboard.md).
- **Broadcast**: one text message sent to many people. Each recipient gets their own conversation, and replies come back to the inbox. See [Messaging workspace](messaging.md).
- **Business hours**: a calendar of opening hours and holidays, used by queues, entry points, the dialer's calling window and automatic follow-ups. See [Business hours](business-hours.md).

## C

- **Cadence**: a series of follow-up messages sent when a customer stops replying to an automated text conversation. See [Cadences](cadences.md).
- **Callback**: a call back to a customer who chose to hang up and keep their place in a queue, instead of waiting. See [Queues](queues.md).
- **Caller ID**: the number a customer sees when you call them. See [Dialer profiles](dialer-profiles.md) and [Omnichannel addresses](channel-endpoints.md).
- **Campaign**: a named push of work, such as *Spring lead drive*. Every activity carries one, agents sign in to a campaign to get its dialer calls, and reports filter by it. See [Campaigns](campaigns.md).
- **Campaign group**: a set of related campaigns rolled up together in reports. See [Campaigns](campaigns.md).
- **Channel**: how you reach a contact: **Phone** or **SMS**.
- **Channel endpoint**: the older name for an *omnichannel address*.
- **Chat interaction**: an ad-hoc AI chat that does not need a profile set up first. See [Chat interactions](ai/chat-interactions.md).
- **Chat widget**: a floating AI chat window, on your public website or on every admin page. See [Chat widgets](ai/chat-widgets.md).
- **Connection**: the link to an AI service, such as OpenAI or Azure OpenAI, that your AI profiles use. See [AI connections](ai/connections.md).
- **Contact**: a person you call or text, such as a customer. Every activity, call and conversation is linked to a contact. See [Contacts](contacts.md).
- **Contact method**: a phone number or email address on a contact. See [Contacts](contacts.md).
- **Conversion**: turning a qualified lead into a contact, optionally with an account and an opportunity. See [Leads, accounts and opportunities](leads-accounts-opportunities.md).

## D

- **Data source**: company data the AI can search before it answers, such as your site's content, a search index, a database table or a website. See [Knowledge](ai/knowledge.md).
- **Deployment**: a specific AI model made available through a connection, such as a chat model. Profiles pick the deployment they use. See [AI connections](ai/connections.md).
- **Dial from**: the number customers see for one dialer load's calls. See [Load activities](load-inventory.md).
- **Dialer modes**: how the dialer places calls. **Preview**: the agent reviews the record, then clicks **Dial**. **Power** and **Progressive**: the system places the calls itself for agents who are available, reserving one agent for each call, and the record opens as the call starts. See [Dialer profiles](dialer-profiles.md).
- **Dialer profile**: the settings for outbound dialing: the mode, the caller ID, the calling window and the compliance rules. See [Dialer profiles](dialer-profiles.md).
- **Disposition**: the outcome of an activity, such as *No answer*, *Call back* or *Sold*. Agents pick one when they complete an activity, and the AI picks one when its conversation ends. See [Dispositions](dispositions.md).
- **Do not call / Do not SMS / Do not email**: a contact's request not to be reached that way. Dialers, loads and automated messages skip them. See [Contacts](contacts.md).
- **Do-not-call registry**: a list of numbers that must not be called, either national (such as the USA FTC registry) or your company's own. See [Do-not-call lists](administration/do-not-call-lists.md).
- **Docked agent bar**: the small bar at the bottom of every admin page that pops up with each offer while an agent is signed in. See [Agent workspace](agent-workspace.md).
- **Document**: a file you upload to an AI profile so it answers from it. See [Knowledge](ai/knowledge.md).

## E

- **Entitlement**: an agent's record of which queues and campaigns they may sign in to, in what order, and which skills they hold. Supervisors need one too. See [Skills and agent entitlements](skills-and-entitlements.md).
- **Entry point**: the front door for one or more of your numbers. It decides where calls or texts go (a queue, one agent or an AI), the opening hours, the phone menu and voicemail. See [Inbound entry points and IVR menus](entry-points-and-ivr.md).
- **Extension**: a short internal number, such as 1001, that belongs to one person. See [Extensions](extensions.md).

## F

- **Feature**: a part of the product that an administrator turns on in **Tools > Features**. Its screens appear only while it is on. See [Features and settings](getting-started/features-and-settings.md).

## H

- **Handoff**: the AI passing a customer to a person in a queue, when the customer asks for one, looks like a qualified lead, or is frustrated. See [Subjects](subjects.md).
- **Hold music**: the audio callers hear while they wait in a queue. See [Voice media](voice-media.md).

## I

- **Interaction**: the record of one call or conversation with a customer, shown in the agent workspace and in reports. See [Agent workspace](agent-workspace.md).
- **Interaction Center**: the admin menu that holds the CRM and the contact center. See [Finding your way](getting-started/finding-your-way.md).
- **IVR menu**: a keypad menu callers hear, such as "For support press 1". Each key sends the caller to a queue, a person, voicemail or another menu. See [Inbound entry points and IVR menus](entry-points-and-ivr.md).

## K

- **Knowledge**: the documents and data sources an AI profile answers from. See [Knowledge](ai/knowledge.md).

## L

- **Lead**: a prospect you have not qualified yet. You call and text it like a contact, but it is kept apart until you convert it. See [Leads, accounts and opportunities](leads-accounts-opportunities.md).
- **Lead source** / **Lead status**: where a lead came from, such as *Trade show*, and where it is in qualification, such as *Working - Contacted*. See [Leads, accounts and opportunities](leads-accounts-opportunities.md).
- **Legal hold**: a mark that stops a recording or voicemail from being deleted. See [Contact Center settings](contact-center-settings.md).
- **Listen**: a supervisor hears a live call silently. See [Live dashboard](live-dashboard.md).
- **Live dashboard**: the supervisor's real-time view of queues and agents. See [Live dashboard](live-dashboard.md).

## M

- **MCP (Model Context Protocol)**: an open standard for connecting AI to outside tools and data. Your site can use tools from MCP servers, and can offer its own as one. See [Tools and agents](ai/tools-and-agents.md).
- **Memory**: what the AI remembers about a signed-in user across conversations, such as their preferences. See [Memory](ai/memory.md).
- **Message template**: a canned response agents insert into a text reply. See [Messaging workspace](messaging.md).
- **Messaging workspace**: the shared inbox for text conversations with customers. See [Messaging workspace](messaging.md).
- **My workspace**: the agent's live desk: presence, queues, offers, the current call and recent work. See [Agent workspace](agent-workspace.md).

## N

- **Number not in service**: a disconnected or invalid number. Calls that reach one are completed on their own, and the number is never loaded or dialed again. See [Numbers not in service](numbers-not-in-service.md).

## O

- **Offer**: work offered to one agent, with a countdown to accept or decline. See [Agent workspace](agent-workspace.md).
- **Omnichannel address**: a phone number your business owns, listed once, with what it is used for: voice calls, text messages or both, and the agents who call and text from it. See [Omnichannel addresses](channel-endpoints.md).
- **Opportunity**: a deal you are working on, with a stage, an amount and a close date. See [Leads, accounts and opportunities](leads-accounts-opportunities.md).
- **Opportunity stage**: a step in your sales pipeline, with a probability, and whether it is won or lost. See [Leads, accounts and opportunities](leads-accounts-opportunities.md).
- **Outcome**: a special meaning on a disposition, such as *Number not in service* or *No answer*, so the platform can apply it on its own. See [Dispositions](dispositions.md).
- **Overflow**: moving a waiting caller to another queue after a time, when the queue is full, or after hours. See [Queues](queues.md).

## P

- **Permission**: the right to do one thing, such as *Manage Contact Center queues*. Roles are bundles of permissions. See [Roles and permissions](getting-started/roles-and-permissions.md).
- **Power dialing**, **Preview dialing**, **Progressive dialing**: see *Dialer modes*.
- **Presence**: whether an agent can take work right now: **Available**, a reason such as *Lunch*, or **Offline**. See [Agent workspace](agent-workspace.md).
- **Prompt template**: a reusable block of AI instructions that profiles can share. See [Prompt templates](ai/prompt-templates.md).

## Q

- **Queue**: where waiting calls and messages line up until an agent takes them. It decides which agent is next, what callers hear, and what happens when you are closed or busy. See [Queues](queues.md).
- **Queue group**: a set of queues reported together, such as *Sales* or *Service*. It does not change routing. See [Queues](queues.md).
- **Quiet hours**: the time outside a queue's business hours. Agents see a warning before texting a customer then. See [Messaging workspace](messaging.md).

## R

- **Reason code**: see *Agent state*.
- **Role**: a named bundle of permissions, such as Agent or Supervisor, given to users. See [Roles and permissions](getting-started/roles-and-permissions.md).
- **Routing strategy**: how a queue picks the next agent: longest idle, round robin, or least busy. See [Queues](queues.md).

## S

- **Secure data capture**: a one-time link an agent sends so the customer can type card or bank details themselves. See [Contact Center settings](contact-center-settings.md).
- **Service level (SLA)**: how long an item may wait in a queue before it counts as a breach. See [Queues](queues.md).
- **Shared voicemail**: a queue's voicemail box, which the team works together. See [Voicemail](voicemail.md).
- **Skill**: something an agent can do, such as *Spanish* or *Billing*, with a proficiency from 1 to 5. Queues can require or prefer skills. See [Skills and agent entitlements](skills-and-entitlements.md).
- **Soft phone**: the phone in your browser, run in the browser extension or the Windows app, where agents make and take calls. See [Soft phone](soft-phone.md).
- **Subject**: what a call or message is about, such as *Support request*, with the fields the agent fills in. It is inbound or outbound. See [Subjects](subjects.md).
- **Subject flow**: what happens after each disposition of a subject: finish, try again later, or create a new activity. See [Subject flows](subject-flows.md).
- **Supervisor**: a team lead who watches the queues, helps on live calls and reads reports. Also the name of a built-in role. See [Roles and permissions](getting-started/roles-and-permissions.md).

## T

- **Tool**: an action the AI can take during a conversation, such as searching your content or calling another system. See [Tools and agents](ai/tools-and-agents.md).
- **Transfer**: handing a call to someone else. **Blind** hands it over at once; **warm** lets you talk to the person first. See [Placing and handling calls](calls.md).

## V

- **Voice media**: your library of audio for calls: hold music and recorded menu prompts. See [Voice media](voice-media.md).
- **Voicemail greeting**: what callers hear when you miss their call. You record your own. See [Voicemail](voicemail.md).

## W

- **Web crawler**: collects pages from a website so the AI can answer from them. See [Knowledge](ai/knowledge.md).
- **Whisper**: a supervisor talks to the agent during a live call without the customer hearing. See [Live dashboard](live-dashboard.md).
- **Workflow**: an automation that starts on an event, such as a call ending, and runs tasks, such as sending a text. See [Contact Center workflows](workflows.md) and [AI workflows](ai/workflows.md).
- **Wrap-up**: the time after a queue or campaign call when the agent finishes notes. It ends when the agent completes the activity. See [Agent workspace](agent-workspace.md).
