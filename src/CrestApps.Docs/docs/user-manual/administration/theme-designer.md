The Theme Designer lets you change how your site looks, its colors, fonts, header, footer and page layouts, without anyone else seeing the changes until you are happy with them. Your changes go to your own **theme draft**. You see the draft while you browse the site and the admin, everyone else sees the published site, and one click publishes the whole draft.

You can ask the AI chat widget to do the work for you, for example "make the site look like a modern bakery, with warm colors and rounded cards", or "put the menu in a dark header with the logo on the left".

## Ask the AI to restyle the site

1. Ask your administrator to give the assistant the admin widget uses the **Theme Designer Agent** on its **Capabilities** tab.
2. Open the chat widget on any admin page and describe what you want: colors, fonts, a site you like, or the pages to change.
3. The assistant writes the change to your draft and tells you what it did.
4. Open the site in another tab. A small dark bar at the bottom left says you are previewing your draft.
5. Ask for more changes until you like it, then publish the draft on **Design > Theme Draft**. If your administrator lets the assistant publish too, it asks you to **Allow** first.

## Review, publish or discard the draft

1. Open **Design > Theme Draft**. The page lists every change: which template or stylesheet, and whether publishing adds, replaces or removes it.
2. Click **View** to compare what is published with your draft, and **Drop** to leave one item as it is.
3. Click **Publish** to make the whole draft live for everyone, or **Discard** to throw it away.
4. Click **Stop preview** to see the published site again while keeping your draft, and **Preview** to see it again.

This page always shows the published admin. If a change breaks how the admin looks, open **Design > Theme Draft** and discard it.

## Site resources

The stylesheets the assistant writes are [site resources](site-resources.md). Saving one on **Design > Site Resources** changes the published site straight away; to try a change first, ask the AI chat widget to make it in your theme draft.
