The **Knowledge Base** is your help center: articles that answer common questions, at `/help` on your site.

## Write an article

1. Turn on **Knowledge Base** under **Configuration > Features**.
2. Go to **Service Desk > Knowledge Base > Articles** and click **New Knowledge Article**.
3. Give it a title and write the body. Choose **Who can read it**: everyone, people who are signed in, or staff only.
4. Give it a **Category** to list it with similar articles, and a short **Summary**. Search looks at the title and the summary.
5. Publish it, or save a draft to finish later.

Staff-only articles are for your team. Give the **View staff-only knowledge articles** permission to the roles that should read them. The same permission shows **Knowledge Base** in the **Service Desk** menu, with your **Articles** and a link to the **Help center**; writing an article still needs the content permissions of the Knowledge Article type.

## The help center

Visitors find the categories, the most helpful articles and a search box at `/help`. Each article asks **Was this helpful?**; the answers show how well it works.
