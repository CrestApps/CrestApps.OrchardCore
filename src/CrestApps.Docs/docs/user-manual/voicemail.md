Voicemail goes to one of two places, chosen on the [inbound entry point](entry-points-and-ivr.md#voicemail):

- **your own inbox**, which you play from the soft phone's **Voicemail** tab;
- a **queue's shared voicemail box**, which the team works from **Interaction Center > Shared voicemail**.

## Record your voicemail greeting

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Menu** | Interaction Center > My voicemail greeting |
| **Permission** | Sign in to queues and campaigns (you also need an agent profile, which is created the first time you sign in) |

<video controls preload="metadata" width="100%" aria-label="Screencast of the My voicemail greeting page with record, upload and preview controls">
  <source src="/img/docs/um-voicemail-greeting.mp4" type="video/mp4" />
</video>

1. Open **Interaction Center > My voicemail greeting**.
2. Click **Record with microphone** and speak your greeting, or **Upload a file** (MP3, WAV, OGG or WebM, up to 20 MB).
3. Click **Preview** to listen, then **Save greeting**.

**Remove greeting** goes back to the entry point's default greeting.

## Listen to your voicemail

Open the soft phone and choose the **Voicemail** tab. A badge shows how many messages are new. Click a message to play it; tick messages and click **Delete** to remove them. Messages under legal hold say so and cannot be deleted.

## Shared voicemail

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Menu** | Interaction Center > Shared voicemail (the menu shows how many are new) |
| **Permissions** | Access shared voicemail (to work the box); Manage shared voicemail (to delete and take over claims). The *Supervisor* role has both. |

<video controls preload="metadata" width="100%" aria-label="Screencast of the shared voicemail list with queue and status filters and the claim, call back and mark done actions">
  <source src="/img/docs/um-shared-voicemail.mp4" type="video/mp4" />
</video>

1. Open **Interaction Center > Shared voicemail**.
2. Filter by queue (*All my queues* by default) and status: **Open** (new and claimed), **New**, **Claimed**, **Done** or **All**.
3. Play a message, then click **I'll handle this** to claim it so the rest of the team knows it is yours.
4. Click **Call back** to dial the caller straight from your own soft phone.
5. When you are finished, click **Mark done** and add an optional note (up to 1000 characters).

Other actions: **Return to queue** releases your claim, **Reopen** brings back a finished message, **Take over** claims a message someone else is holding (manage permission), and **Delete** removes it (manage permission; not allowed under legal hold).

You see the queues on your own [entitlement record](skills-and-entitlements.md); users with *Manage the Contact Center* see every queue.
