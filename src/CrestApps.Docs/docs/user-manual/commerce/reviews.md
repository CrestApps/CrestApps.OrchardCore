**Product reviews** let customers rate a product they bought from 1 to 5 stars and say what they think. You approve each review before it shows. Approved reviews appear on the product's page with its stars, and shoppers can sort your listings by customer rating.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Menu** | Commerce > Reviews; Settings > Commerce > Reviews |
| **Permission** | **Moderate product reviews** to approve reviews; **Review products bought** to write one (signed-in users have it) |
| **Feature** | Product Reviews |

<AskYourAdmin />

## How customers review

At the bottom of a product's page, **Customer reviews** shows the approved reviews, newest first, and the product's average rating. A signed-in customer who bought the product sees **Write a review**: they choose their stars, and can add a title, a few words and the name shown with the review. Reviews from buyers are marked **Verified purchase**.

After sending, the customer is told their review will show once you approve it. They can change it later; a changed review waits for approval again. Each customer has one review per product.

Under the product's price, its stars, average and number of reviews link to the reviews.

## Approve reviews

**Commerce > Reviews** lists the reviews waiting for approval, newest first. Each shows the product, who wrote it, the stars, the title and text, and whether it's a verified purchase.

- **Approve** shows the review on the product and counts it in its rating.
- **Reject** keeps it off the product.
- **Delete** removes it.

Use the status filter to see **Approved**, **Rejected** or all reviews, and change your mind on any of them.

## Ratings in listings

Once a product has an approved review, shoppers can sort a listing by **Customer rating: highest first**, and listings that choose their filters automatically offer a **Customer rating** filter with *4 and up*, *3 and up* and *2 and up*. To add them to a listing that has its own filters and sorts, edit the listing and pick **Customer rating**.

## Settings

Under **Settings > Commerce > Reviews**:

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Approve reviews before they show** | On by default. Untick it to show reviews as soon as they're sent. |
| **Only buyers can review** | On by default. Untick it to let any signed-in user review a product; their reviews aren't marked as verified purchases. |
