**Product reviews** let customers rate a product they bought from 1 to 5 stars and say what they think. You approve each review before it shows. Approved reviews appear on the product's page with its stars, and shoppers can sort your listings by customer rating.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Menu** | Commerce > Reviews; Settings > Commerce > Reviews |
| **Permission** | **Moderate product reviews** to approve reviews; **Review products bought** to write one (signed-in users have it) |
| **Feature** | Product Reviews |

## How customers review

<YouTube video="reviews" title="Video of what shoppers see on a product page" start={86} end={122} />

At the bottom of a product's page, **Customer reviews** shows the approved reviews, newest first, and the product's average rating. A signed-in customer who bought the product sees **Write a review**: they choose **Your rating**, and can add a **Title**, **Your review** and the **Name shown with your review**. Reviews from buyers are marked **Verified purchase**. A visitor who isn't signed in is asked to sign in, and, when only buyers can review, a customer who hasn't bought the product is told so.

<YouTube video="reviews" title="Video of writing a review" start={122} end={158} />

After they click **Send review**, the customer is told their review will show once you approve it. They can change it later; a changed review waits for approval again. Each customer has one review per product.

Under the product's price, its stars, average and number of reviews link to the reviews.

## Photos

With the **Media** feature on, buyers can add up to three photos to their review: JPEG, PNG or WebP images of up to 5 MB each. They show with the review, on the product's page and under **Commerce > Reviews**. New photos replace the ones a review had.

## Ask buyers for reviews

<YouTube video="reviews" title="Video of the review settings" start={200} end={250} />

Under **Settings > Commerce > Reviews**, tick **Ask buyers for reviews** and choose the **Days after paying**, such as 14. Each order's buyer then gets one email with a link to review each product they bought. Only buyers who said yes to news and offers are asked, and only for orders from the last 60 days. It needs your site's email, its base URL, and Customers.

## Approve reviews

<YouTube video="reviews" title="Video of approving, rejecting and deleting reviews" start={158} end={200} />

**Commerce > Reviews** lists the reviews waiting for approval, newest first. Each shows the product, who wrote it, the stars, the title and text, and whether it's a verified purchase.

- **Approve** shows the review on the product and counts it in its rating.
- **Reject** keeps it off the product.
- **Delete** removes it.

Use the status filter to see **Approved**, **Rejected** or all reviews, and change your mind on any of them.

## Ratings in listings

<YouTube video="reviews" title="Video of sorting and filtering a listing by customer rating" start={250} end={285} />

Once a product has an approved review, shoppers can sort a listing by **Customer rating: highest first**, and listings that choose their filters automatically offer a **Customer rating** filter with *4 and up*, *3 and up* and *2 and up*. To add them to a listing that has its own filters and sorts, edit the listing, open **Choose filters** and tick **Customer rating**, and do the same under **Choose sorts**.

## Settings

Under **Settings > Commerce > Reviews**:

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Approve reviews before they show** | On by default. Untick it to show reviews as soon as they're sent. |
| **Only buyers can review** | On by default. Untick it to let any signed-in user review a product; their reviews aren't marked as verified purchases. |
