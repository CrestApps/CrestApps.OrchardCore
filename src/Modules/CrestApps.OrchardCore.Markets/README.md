# Markets

Sells to several countries, each market with its own currency, exchange rate and price rounding, language, and prices with or without tax. Shoppers pick their market on the storefront, and see and pay prices in its currency.

## Features

| Feature | Id | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Markets | `CrestApps.OrchardCore.Markets` | Sells to several countries, each market with its own currency, exchange rate and price rounding, language, and prices with or without tax. Shoppers pick their market on the storefront, and see and pay prices in its currency. |

Enable a feature under **Configuration > Features**, or with the `Feature` step of a recipe.
