# Product Reviews

Lets buyers rate and review the products they bought, moderated by the store under Commerce > Reviews, with stars on product pages and an average rating listings can filter and sort by.

## Features

| Feature | Id | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product Reviews | `CrestApps.OrchardCore.Reviews` | Lets buyers rate and review the products they bought, moderated by the store under Commerce > Reviews, with stars on product pages and an average rating listings can filter and sort by. |

Enable a feature under **Configuration > Features**, or with the `Feature` step of a recipe.
