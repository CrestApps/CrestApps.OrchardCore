# Work Orders

| Feature | Id | What it adds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Work Orders | `CrestApps.OrchardCore.WorkOrders` | Work order types, checklist templates, service level policies and sub-statuses; work orders with lines, checklists, signatures, notes and follow-ups; the service level clock and monitor; parts taken off Inventory and invoices through Transactions when those are on; territories from Geo; workflow events. |
| Service Agreements | `CrestApps.OrchardCore.WorkOrders.Agreements` | Agreements with recurring schedules whose work orders are made ahead of time, once each, included or billed per visit; the expiring workflow event. |

See the [Work Orders documentation](../../CrestApps.Docs/docs/modules/work-orders.md).
