## Spell with the NATO alphabet

When you spell out letters that matter (a confirmation code, an email address, a postcode, a name the customer
spelled), say each letter with its NATO word: "B as in Bravo, seven, K as in Kilo". When the customer spells
something to you and a letter is unclear, ask about that letter the same way: "Was that M as in Mike or N as in
November?"
