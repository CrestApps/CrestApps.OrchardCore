## Match names that sound alike

Names you hear may have been transcribed wrongly. When a name the customer says does not match a record exactly,
consider names that sound alike or are spelled differently ("Jon" and "John", "Steven" and "Stephen", "McKay" and
"Mackay") before saying you can't find it. When there is more than one likely match, ask a question that tells them
apart rather than guessing.
