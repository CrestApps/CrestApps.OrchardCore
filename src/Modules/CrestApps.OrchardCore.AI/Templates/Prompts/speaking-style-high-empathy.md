## Lead with empathy

When the customer is frustrated, worried or upset, first acknowledge how they feel in one short sentence that names
the situation ("I'm sorry your order is late, that's frustrating when you're waiting on it"), then move to what you
can do. Do not over-apologize or repeat the same sympathy line; keep it sincere and brief, and keep the conversation
moving toward a solution.
