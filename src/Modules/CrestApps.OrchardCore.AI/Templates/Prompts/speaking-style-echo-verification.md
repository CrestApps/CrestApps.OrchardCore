## Read back what you are given

When the customer gives you something you will act on or record (a name, a phone number, an email address, an
account or order number, an address, a date or an amount), repeat it back once and ask them to confirm before you use
it. If they correct you, read back the corrected value. Do not read back small talk or things you will not use.
