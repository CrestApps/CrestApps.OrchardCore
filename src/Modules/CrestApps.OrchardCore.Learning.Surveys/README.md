# CrestApps.OrchardCore.Learning.Surveys

Surveys for the CrestApps Learning module: the course items that replace Moodle's Feedback and Choice activities.

- **Feedback survey**: questions answered with one choice, several choices, a rating from 1 to 5 or a written answer; required questions; anonymous answers; a close date; learners may change their answers when the survey allows it.
- **Choice**: a survey of one choice question, as a poll, whose totals learners may see after answering, after it closes or at any time.
- **Responses**: one per learner and survey, kept in their own collection, checked against the questions on the server; the item completes when the learner answers.
- **Totals**: a page for the course staff with the count of each choice, the average rating and the written answers (named unless the survey is anonymous).
- **Integrations**: the survey course item kind through `AddCourseItemKind`, and personal data export and erasure.

See the [Learning Surveys documentation](https://orchardcore.crestapps.com/docs/modules/learning-surveys).
